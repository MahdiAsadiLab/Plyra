About these numbers

ChessLens Accuracy and the move classifications are custom, experimental metrics made for this project. They are not Chess.com's (or Lichess's) official Accuracy or Game Review classifications. Labels depend on engine evaluations at a fixed depth; special labels (Great, Brilliant, Miss, Book) are conservative heuristics.
